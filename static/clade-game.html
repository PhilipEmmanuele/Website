About the tree & sources · reviewed October 2026

This is a curated, pruned animal tree. Answers are the closest named group included here; real lineages often have intermediate clades. Branch lengths do not represent time. Some deep relationships remain debated, including the root of placental mammals and relationships within Neoaves and Arachnida. Multifurcations here represent omitted or unresolved branches, not simultaneous splits.

Classification cross-checks: NCBI Taxonomy, Mammal Diversity Database, WoRMS, and NCBI bird classification update. Names and classifications can change; this is a reviewed snapshot, not a live database.
